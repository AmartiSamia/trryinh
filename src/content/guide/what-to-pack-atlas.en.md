On our treks, mules carry the main bags, so you only walk with a daypack. That makes packing simpler, but a few things really matter in the mountains.

## On your feet

- **Walking boots with good grip**, already worn in. Paths are rocky and loose, especially on Toubkal.
- **Two or three pairs of walking socks.**
- Light shoes or sandals for the evening.

## Layers, not one big coat

Mountain weather changes quickly, and it can be 25 °C in Imlil and below zero at dawn on the summit, on the same day.

- A breathable T-shirt for walking
- A fleece and a light down jacket
- A waterproof, windproof jacket
- Warm hat and gloves (even in summer for Toubkal)
- Walking trousers; a pair of warm leggings for the nights

## In your daypack

- Water bottle (1.5 L) or water bladder
- Sun hat, sunglasses and sunscreen: the sun is strong at altitude
- Head torch with spare batteries (summit day starts in the dark)
- Snacks you like
- Small first-aid kit with your personal medicine
- Some cash in dirhams for drinks and tips

## For the nights

Guesthouses and refuges provide blankets, but a **sleeping bag or a liner** is more comfortable and hygienic. Tell us if you need to rent one.

## What you do not need

Food, cooking gear, tents on camping treks and the main luggage are carried by the team. Leave valuables you do not need at your hotel in Marrakech.

## In winter

From December to March, add full winter clothing and boots that take crampons. Crampons and an ice axe are part of our winter trip.
