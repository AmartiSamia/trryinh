Almost everyone who comes to Marrakech wants a night in the desert. There are three realistic options, and the right one depends mostly on how much time you have.

## Merzouga: the big dunes

Erg Chebbi, near Merzouga, is the postcard Sahara: orange dunes more than 100 m high, changing colour with the light. It is also far, around 560 km from Marrakech, so you need **three days**: two long days of driving through the Atlas, the gorges and the kasbahs, with one night in the dunes in between.

Choose Merzouga if you want real dunes and you enjoy the road itself. Every hour the landscape changes: the Tichka pass, Aït Benhaddou, the Dades and Todra gorges.

## Zagora: the short option

Zagora is closer, about 360 km away, and possible in **two days** with one night at camp. The road through the Draa valley and its palm groves is beautiful, but the dunes are small. It is a good choice if you only have two days and mostly want the experience of a desert camp and a starry sky.

## Agafay: the desert in an evening

Agafay, 45 minutes from Marrakech, is a stony desert of bare golden hills, not sand. With a camel ride at sunset and dinner at a camp, it is perfect if you have only one evening, or travel with young children.

## Quick comparison

| | Merzouga | Zagora | Agafay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time needed | 3 days | 2 days | Half a day |
| Driving | Long | Medium | Short |
| Sand dunes | Large | Small | None |
| Best for | The real Sahara | A short desert night | An evening out |

## When to go

March to May and October to November are the best months. Summer days pass 40 °C, and winter nights in the desert are close to freezing, so bring a warm layer.
